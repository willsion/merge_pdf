## Small Things Worth Knowing Before You Click Merge

Give your files clear names before you upload them. When you're arranging thumbnails, "final_draft.pdf" beats "document3.pdf" every time.

If you're combining a lot of files, do it in stages — merge a few at a time, then merge the results. It keeps the thumbnails manageable and makes it easier to spot a mistake.

And check the order once, right before you click. Re-downloading because two pages are swapped is a classic time-waster, and this tool makes the check a ten-second glance at the thumbnails.

It's also worth saying what this tool isn't. It's not a PDF editor, it won't sign documents, and it won't run OCR on your scans — other pages on the site handle those jobs.
